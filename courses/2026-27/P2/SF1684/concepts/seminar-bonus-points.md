## What to attend

The six SF1684 seminars begin with an optional written test. Each test has two problems in 30 minutes. Each problem earns either zero or one point; both the method and answer must be correct. After a 20-minute break, the teacher explains the solutions.

The current published schedule is:

- **2 November, 15:00–17:00:** Vector geometry. Test 15:00–15:30.
- **9 November, 13:00–15:00:** Linear systems and matrices. Test 13:00–13:30.
- **16 November, 13:00–15:00:** Solution sets, determinants, and volumes. Test 13:00–13:30.
- **23 November, 15:00–17:00:** Linear transformations and eigenvalues. Test 15:00–15:30.
- **30 November, 13:00–15:00:** Bases, projections, and least squares. Test 13:00–13:30.
- **7 December, 13:00–15:00:** Diagonalization and quadratic forms. Test 13:00–13:30.

Times are Stockholm local time. The public timetable does not provide rooms; check your current Canvas group and official timetable. Arrive before the test starts. The course does not offer a replacement test if you miss a seminar.

## Bring physical photo ID

Keep valid physical photo identification visible at your seat throughout the test. Digital ID and student cards are not accepted. FUNKA students with approved extended time should inform the teaching assistant; the specified extension is 25%, or 7 minutes 30 seconds.

## How the bonus is calculated

Add your scores across the six tests, then use the conversion:

- 0 test points → 0 exam bonus points.
- 1–2 → 1; 3–4 → 2; 5–6 → 3.
- 7–8 → 4; 9–10 → 5; 11–12 → 6.

The bonus is added to **Part 1** of the written exam, with a combined cap of **18 points** for that part. It is valid for the current academic year, through July. Check your recorded points in Canvas and resolve errors with the teaching assistant before the exam.

## Prepare for the next test

Start with the preceding module's official reading guide and workshop problems. This is a preparation route, not a promise about the exact test questions.

For the first seminar, revisit [vectors and length](/courses/sf1684/concepts/vectors-and-length), [dot and cross products](/courses/sf1684/concepts/dot-and-cross-products), and [lines, planes, and projection](/courses/sf1684/concepts/lines-planes-and-projection).

For the second seminar, prepare [linear systems and elimination](/courses/sf1684/concepts/linear-systems-and-elimination), [matrix arithmetic](/courses/sf1684/concepts/matrix-arithmetic), and [inverses](/courses/sf1684/concepts/matrix-inverses). Attempt the official [Module 2 workshop problems](https://canvas.kth.se/courses/65269/files/10687614) before reading the solutions.

All six tests are linked from [the deadlines page](/deadlines). Ordinary workshops provide practice; simply attending a workshop is not one of these bonus tests.
