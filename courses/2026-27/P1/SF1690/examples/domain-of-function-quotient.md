Let $f(x)=\sqrt{x}$ and $g(x)=\sqrt{1-x}$. Their shared domain is $[0,1]$. For $f/g$, also require $g(x)\ne0$, so exclude $x=1$. Therefore $D(f/g)=[0,1)$.
