Let $f(x)=3x-2$ on $\mathbb R$. Solving $y=3x-2$ gives $x=(y+2)/3$, so $f^{-1}(x)=(x+2)/3$.

Both functions have domain and range $\mathbb R$. Check:

$$f^{-1}(f(x))=\frac{3x-2+2}{3}=x.$$

$$f(f^{-1}(x))=3\frac{x+2}{3}-2=x.$$

For instance, $f(4)=10$, while $f^{-1}(10)=4$. The reciprocal $1/(3x-2)$ does not undo this mapping.
