Start with

$$x^2+y^2+2x-3y=3.$$

Complete each square:

$$x^2+2x=(x+1)^2-1,$$

$$y^2-3y=\left(y-\frac32\right)^2-\frac94.$$

Thus

$$\left(x+1\right)^2+\left(y-\frac32\right)^2=3+1+\frac94=\frac{25}{4}.$$

The circle has centre $(-1,3/2)$ and radius $5/2$.
