From $x^2+y^2=1$, solving for $y$ gives $y=\pm\sqrt{1-x^2}$. The full circle is not one function of $x$, but $f(x)=\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and $g(x)=-\sqrt{1-x^2}$ are two functions on $[-1,1]$.
