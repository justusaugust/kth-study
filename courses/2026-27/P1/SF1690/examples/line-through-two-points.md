For $A=(3,-3)$ and $B=(-1,2)$, $$m=\frac{2-(-3)}{-1-3}=-\frac54.$$ Using point-slope form through $A$, $$y+3=-\frac54(x-3).$$ Therefore $$y=-\frac54x+\frac34.$$ Substituting either original point verifies the equation.
