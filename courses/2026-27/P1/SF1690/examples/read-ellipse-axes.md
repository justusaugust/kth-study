For

$$\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1,$$

the semiaxes are $a=3$ and $b=2$. The ellipse is centred at the origin, with vertices $(\pm3,0)$ and co-vertices $(0,\pm2)$.

Because

$$c=\sqrt{a^2-b^2}=\sqrt5,$$

the foci are $(\pm\sqrt5,0)$. The major-axis length is $2a=6$, which is also the constant sum of distances from any point on the ellipse to the two foci.
