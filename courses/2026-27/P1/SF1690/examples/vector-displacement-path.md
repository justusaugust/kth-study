Let $A=(1,-2)$, $B=(4,2)$ and $C=(-2,5)$. Find the displacement along $A\to B\to C$, and distinguish it from the distance travelled.

First subtract start from end on each leg:

$$
\overrightarrow{AB}=(4-1,2-(-2))=(3,4),\qquad
\overrightarrow{BC}=(-2-4,5-2)=(-6,3).
$$

The total displacement is $(3,4)+(-6,3)=(-3,7)$. Directly, $C-A=(-2-1,5-(-2))=(-3,7)$, which checks the result.

The displacement length is $\sqrt{(-3)^2+7^2}=\sqrt{58}$. Travelling along both straight segments instead covers

$$
\|\overrightarrow{AB}\|+\|\overrightarrow{BC}\|=5+\sqrt{45}=5+3\sqrt5.
$$

These lengths differ because the path bends at $B$. The vector sum records the net change of position, not the total path length.
