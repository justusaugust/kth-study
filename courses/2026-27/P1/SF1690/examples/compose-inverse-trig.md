Find $\tan(\arccos(-3/5))$ exactly.

Let $\theta=\arccos(-3/5)$. Its principal interval is $[0,\pi]$, and its cosine is negative, so $\theta$ is in quadrant II. Therefore sine is positive:

$$\sin\theta=\sqrt{1-9/25}=4/5.$$

Then $\tan\theta=(4/5)/(-3/5)=-4/3$. A triangle with unsigned side lengths alone would lose the negative sign; the principal interval supplies it.
