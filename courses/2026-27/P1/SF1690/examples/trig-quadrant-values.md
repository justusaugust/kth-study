Find sine, cosine and tangent at $5\pi/6$.

The reference angle is $\pi/6$. In the second quadrant the horizontal coordinate is negative and the vertical coordinate positive:

$$
\cos(5\pi/6)=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\qquad \sin(5\pi/6)=\frac12.
$$

Therefore $\tan(5\pi/6)=(1/2)/(-\sqrt3/2)=-1/\sqrt3$. The signs follow from geometry, not from a memorised table of positive values.
