For the line $4x+6y=15$, set $y=0$ to find the $x$-intercept:

$$4x=15\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=\frac{15}{4}.$$

Set $x=0$ to find the $y$-intercept:

$$6y=15\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=\frac{15}{6}=\frac52.$$

The intercept points are therefore $(15/4,0)$ and $(0,5/2)$.
