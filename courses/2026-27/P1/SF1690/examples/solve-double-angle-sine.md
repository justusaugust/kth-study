Solve $\sin(2x)=1/2$ for $0\le x<2\pi$.

The angle $2x$ must equal $\pi/6+2k\pi$ or $5\pi/6+2k\pi$. Dividing both families by two gives:

$$
x=\pi/12+k\pi \quad\text{or}\quad x=5\pi/12+k\pi.
$$

The requested interval permits $k=0,1$, so the four solutions are $\pi/12,5\pi/12,13\pi/12,17\pi/12$. Doubling each value and applying periodicity checks the original equation. Stopping after two solutions misses the second cycle.
