Let the focus be $F=(0,p)$ and the directrix be $y=-p$. For $P=(x,y)$, the closest directrix point is $Q=(x,-p)$. The parabola condition is

$$\sqrt{x^2+(y-p)^2}=|y+p|.$$

Squaring gives

$$x^2+y^2-2py+p^2=y^2+2py+p^2.$$

After cancellation,

$$x^2=4py.$$

For $p=2$, the equation is $x^2=8y$, the focus is $(0,2)$, and the directrix is $y=-2$.
