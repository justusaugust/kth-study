For $f(u)=\sqrt{u}$ and $g(x)=x+1$, $(f\circ g)(x)=\sqrt{x+1}$ with domain $[-1,\infty)$. Reversing the order gives $(g\circ f)(x)=\sqrt{x}+1$ with domain $[0,\infty)$. The functions differ in both rule and domain.
