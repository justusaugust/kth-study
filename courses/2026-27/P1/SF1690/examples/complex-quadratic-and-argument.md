Solve $z^2+2z+2=0$ and describe each root geometrically.

Complete the square: $(z+1)^2=-1$. Thus $z=-1+i$ or $z=-1-i$. Both have modulus $\sqrt2$.

The first lies in quadrant II, so its principal argument in $(-\pi,\pi]$ is $3\pi/4$. The second lies in quadrant III, so its principal argument is $-3\pi/4$. Therefore

$$
z_1=\sqrt2\bigl(\cos(3\pi/4)+i\sin(3\pi/4)\bigr),\qquad
z_2=\sqrt2\bigl(\cos(-3\pi/4)+i\sin(-3\pi/4)\bigr).
$$

They are reflections across the real axis. Their sum is $-2$ and product is $2$, matching the coefficients of the original quadratic.
