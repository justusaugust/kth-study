For $|3x-2|=4$, split into $3x-2=-4$ or $3x-2=4$, giving $x=-\frac23$ or $x=2$. For $|5x+1|\le1$, write $-1\le5x+1\le1$. Subtract one and divide by five: $-2\le5x\le0$, hence $$x\in\left[-\frac25,0\right].$$
