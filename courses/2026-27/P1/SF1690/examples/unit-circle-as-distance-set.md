A point $(x,y)$ lies on the unit circle exactly when its distance from the origin is one: $$\sqrt{(x-0)^2+(y-0)^2}=1.$$ Squaring both sides gives $x^2+y^2=1$. Replacing $=$ with $<$ selects points closer than one unit (the interior); replacing it with $>$ selects the exterior.
