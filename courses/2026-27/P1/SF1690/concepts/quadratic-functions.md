A quadratic function has the form $f(x)=ax^2+bx+c$ with $a\ne0$.

- $a$ controls the direction and width of the opening.
- $b$ shifts the axis of symmetry, which lies at $x=-\frac{b}{2a}$.
- $c=f(0)$ is the vertical-axis intercept.

Completing the square rewrites the same function in vertex form and makes its turning point visible.
