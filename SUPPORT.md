# KTH Study support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/justusaugust/kth-study/issues/new) to report a bug, incorrect study content, accessibility problem, or plugin failure.

Include the course code, page or plugin action, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot is useful when it does not contain private information.

Do not post credentials, student identifiers, private Canvas material, graded work, or other personal information in an issue.

KTH Study is an independent study aid. For registration, examination enrolment, official deadlines, grades, or account access, contact the relevant KTH service or course team.
