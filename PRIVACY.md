# KTH Study privacy policy

Effective 1 September 2026.

KTH Study is an independent study aid operated by Justus August. It is not an official KTH service.

## Data KTH Study processes

KTH Study does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, student identifiers, credentials, payment information, health information, or other sensitive personal data.

When you use the KTH Study plugin, OpenAI sends the minimum tool input needed for the request, such as a search term, course code, or curriculum item ID, to the KTH Study MCP server. The server returns the matching course material, dates, questions, or interactive visual. KTH Study does not receive your full ChatGPT conversation unless part of it is included in a tool input.

The public corpus contains original study material and links to course sources. It excludes private Canvas files, recordings, classroom photographs, personal administration, and local study state.

## Purpose and legal basis

Tool inputs are processed only to answer the request, render the selected study visual, operate the service, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures. Processing is necessary to provide the service you requested and to maintain its security and reliability.

## Storage and retention

KTH Study does not create user profiles, sell personal data, use advertising trackers, or persist tool inputs or results in its application data. Hosting providers may temporarily process basic network and error information needed to deliver and secure requests. Any diagnostic logs controlled by KTH Study are retained for no more than 30 days.

## Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers needed to deliver the plugin, including OpenAI and the infrastructure provider hosting the MCP server. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. KTH Study does not sell personal data or share it with advertisers.

## Your choices and rights

Do not include personal, confidential, or restricted information in KTH Study requests. You can stop using or uninstall the plugin at any time. Because KTH Study does not maintain user accounts or a user-content database, it normally has no stored user record to export or delete.

For a privacy question or request, use the [KTH Study support page](SUPPORT.md). Do not post credentials, private course material, or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

## Changes

Material changes will be published in this repository with a revised effective date.
